## Creative quality gates

Premium final production now requires both:

1. anti-generic QA, pass threshold 80; and
2. creative benchmark, pass threshold **82** with zero factual/readability blockers.

The benchmark checks:

- hierarchy
- composition variety
- color discipline
- purposeful materiality
- motion purpose
- transition continuity
- hero/payoff moment
- depth/spatial staging
- typography as composition
- readability/safe areas
- factual separation

Automatic blockers include generated documentary evidence, invented official maps/vehicles, unverified testimony, factual data baked into generative pixels, safe-area failure and unreadable critical copy.

A validator pass is a floor, not proof of beauty. Representative rendered frames must also be visually inspected.
